# Review marketing performance

Read the [reporting workflow](../reporting/references/reporting-workflow.md) and [measurement rules](../reporting/references/measurement.md).

Use the requested period and comparison. For an unspecified weekly review, default to the previous completed Monday–Sunday and the week before it. Select the requested sources from the relevant workspace; do not pull every connected platform automatically.

1. Discover the fields for spend, impressions, clicks, conversions, and attributed revenue as available. Use the user's conversion definition or label the platform's returned definition.
2. Retrieve comparable period totals. If the user asks why performance moved, retrieve campaign-level or daily data to locate which segments contributed to the change. Keep total and detail reports separate to avoid double counting.
3. Compute aggregate ratios from the underlying totals. Keep currencies and incompatible attribution definitions separate.
4. Lead with the most consequential finding. Provide a compact scorecard with current value, prior value, and change, followed by the campaigns or channels driving it.
5. Recommend actions proportional to the evidence. Tie each recommendation to an observed value, flag low volume or missing tracking context, and distinguish a diagnostic hypothesis from a verified cause.

Public competitor-ad libraries cannot answer questions about the user's spend or conversions. Route “my Meta ads” to connected-account reporting even if a brand is named. This workflow analyzes performance; it does not change campaigns or verify tracking configuration through tools that Adzviser does not expose.
